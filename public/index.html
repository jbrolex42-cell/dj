UNDERSTAND YOUR OPTIONS

Different settings.
Different kinds of support.

Care types can overlap, and services vary by provider. Use this guide to prepare questions and confirm services directly.

☼

Independent living

For people who generally manage daily activities themselves and want community, convenience, or household services.

Ask about: meals, housekeeping, transportation, and changing needs.
⌂

Adult family homes

Smaller residential settings that may provide hands-on help and daily support, depending on licensing, staffing, and capabilities.

Ask about: caregiver coverage, care plans, rooms, and visits.
✦

Assisted living

Residential communities that may combine housing, meals, activities, and assistance with some daily living tasks.

Ask about: staffing, included services, additional fees, and care limits.
＋

Nursing homes

Licensed facilities that provide skilled nursing and rehabilitation for people who need that level of care.

Ask about: clinical services, rehabilitation, coverage, and admission criteria.
♡

Memory care

Specialized support for people living with Alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia. Services vary by setting.

Ask about: staff training, safety, routines, and individual support.
i
Questions about paying for care?

Private pay, long-term-care insurance, Medicaid, veterans’ benefits, and public programs have different rules. Confirm accepted payment methods, eligibility, and coverage with the provider and relevant program.
